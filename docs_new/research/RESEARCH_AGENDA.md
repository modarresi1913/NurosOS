# NurosOS Research Agenda

## Near-Term (0-6 months)

### R1: Epistemic Integrity Verification
**Question**: Can we formally verify that no epistemic violation goes undetected?
**Approach**: Model the epistemic transition graph as a formal system and prove completeness of forbidden transition detection.
**Deliverable**: Formal verification of epistemic kernel, test suite with 100% transition coverage.

### R2: Memory Decay Dynamics
**Question**: What decay functions produce the most useful memory profiles?
**Approach**: Compare exponential, power-law, and adaptive decay across synthetic environments.
**Deliverable**: Parameterized decay framework, benchmark results.

### R3: Homeostatic Regulation Convergence
**Question**: Under what conditions does the homeostatic kernel converge to stable states?
**Approach**: Analyze regulation dynamics as a dynamical system, characterize basins of attraction.
**Deliverable**: Convergence proofs for simple cases, empirical characterization for complex cases.

### R4: Organism Ladder Validation
**Question**: Does each organism level genuinely exhibit emergent capabilities not present at lower levels?
**Approach**: Design capability tests that are passed at level N but fail at level N-1.
**Deliverable**: Capability differentiation test suite.

## Medium-Term (6-18 months)

### R5: Developmental Plasticity Laws
**Question**: What plasticity rules produce the most adaptive developmental trajectories?
**Approach**: Systematic exploration of Hebbian, anti-Hebbian, reward-modulated, and predictive plasticity rules.
**Deliverable**: Plasticity rule benchmark, recommended defaults per organism level.

### R6: Self-Model Accuracy Bounds
**Question**: What are the fundamental limits on self-model accuracy?
**Approach**: Information-theoretic analysis of self-referential systems.
**Deliverable**: Theoretical bounds, empirical measurements.

### R7: Imagination-Safety Integration
**Question**: How can we ensure that imagination never produces unsafe outcomes while remaining useful?
**Approach**: Design risk estimation for counterfactual simulations, prove safety guarantees.
**Deliverable**: Safe imagination framework, risk estimation benchmarks.

### R8: Reproducibility Framework
**Question**: Can we guarantee that identical genome + experience + runtime = identical behavior?
**Approach**: Hash-based reproducibility verification, deterministic execution mode.
**Deliverable**: Reproducibility verification tool, reproducibility reports.

## Long-Term (18+ months)

### R9: Artificial Ontogenesis
**Question**: Can organisms undergo genuine developmental trajectories analogous to biological development?
**Approach**: Design developmental milestones, study stage transitions, measure developmental trajectories.
**Deliverable**: Developmental milestone framework, trajectory analysis tools.

### R10: Multi-Organism Ecosystems
**Question**: What emergent behaviors arise from populations of interacting organisms?
**Approach**: Design organism ecosystems, study communication, cooperation, competition.
**Deliverable**: Ecosystem simulation framework, emergent behavior catalog.

### R11: Value Alignment Dynamics
**Question**: How do value systems evolve through experience, and can they remain aligned?
**Approach**: Study value drift under various experience distributions.
**Deliverable**: Value drift measurement, alignment preservation mechanisms.

### R12: Synthetic Consciousness Indicators
**Question**: Are there measurable indicators that could distinguish conscious-like from non-conscious-like systems?
**Approach**: Design consciousness-indicator tests based on Integrated Information Theory, Global Workspace Theory, and Higher-Order theories.
**Deliverable**: Consciousness-indicator test suite, results for each organism level.

## Open Questions

1. What is the minimum organism level required for genuine goal-directed behavior?
2. Can self-models achieve stable self-reference without paradox?
3. What role does embodiment play in the development of spatial reasoning?
4. Can artificial dreaming (offline replay) improve learning efficiency?
5. How should responsibility be attributed in multi-organism systems?
